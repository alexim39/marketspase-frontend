Live payout guardrails

Campaign click tracking pays promoters using the campaign or promotion CPC snapshot. This page changes the default for new campaigns without rewriting previous campaign economics.

  • Verified billable clicks debit the marketer wallet and credit the promoter wallet with this CPC.
  • Duplicate, bot, preview, invalid, or fraud-blocked clicks remain non-billable.
  • Existing campaign records keep their stored CPC to avoid surprise budget changes.
