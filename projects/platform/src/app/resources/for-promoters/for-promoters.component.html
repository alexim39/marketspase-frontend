Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about promoting with tracked links

How much can I earn per promotion?

add

Your earnings depend on campaign payout rules, valid tracked clicks, conversions, product orders where applicable, and your account-quality history. Promoters with clean traffic and strong performance can receive better opportunities over time.

What happens if my clicks are suspicious?

add

Suspicious traffic, repeated self-clicks, bots, fake referrals, or misleading sharing can reduce your earnings, lower your rating, limit campaign access, or lead to suspension.

How does MarketSpase know activity came from me?

add

Each accepted promotion or product gives you a unique MarketSpase tracking link. That link connects valid clicks, conversions, product orders, and earnings back to your promoter account.


Keep the link unchanged, follow the campaign instructions, and share only with real audiences to protect your account health.


In the case of disputes, our support team reviews the tracking records and account activity before making a final decision.

When do I get paid?

add

Eligible earnings are reflected in your wallet after valid activity passes campaign rules, quality checks, and payout processing. You can then request withdrawal.

Can I promote multiple offers at once?

add

Yes. You can promote multiple campaigns or products, as long as you follow each campaign rule, keep links separate, and avoid spam or suspicious traffic.

Is there any cost to join?

add

No, joining as a promoter is completely free. You earn from valid activity; there are no fees or hidden charges to join.
