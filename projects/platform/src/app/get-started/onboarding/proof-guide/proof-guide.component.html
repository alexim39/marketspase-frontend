Promoter Success Guide

Use the current tracked-link workflow so your promotion is credited correctly from the start.

check_circle Accept campaigns that fit your audience and review the campaign rules before you share.
check_circle Work from Dashboard > Campaigns > Promotions to download media, copy the caption, and copy your tracked link.
check_circle Share only the generated MarketSpase link and keep the UPI or required campaign details intact.
check_circle Monitor tracked clicks, billable clicks, status, and earnings from the promotion details page.
check_circle Avoid fake clicks, self-clicks, spam traffic, or edited links. Fraud signals can pause the promotion and restrict your account.
